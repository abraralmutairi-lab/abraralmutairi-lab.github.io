SmartProjectManager

Graduation projectTeam of 8Qassim University

A system where managers send projects to employees, employees submit their work, and AI helps evaluate it.

My role:

  • Built the project input, submission and evaluation buttons for the manager and employee interfaces, and linked them to add, view, delete and evaluate.
  • Styled the buttons (colors, sizes, effects).
  • Added the manager control panel: send projects to employees, view evaluations and select the best one.

Challenge solved: projects were reaching the wrong employees because of standard serial IDs. I replaced them with UUIDs and added an employeeId field, so each employee now sees only their own projects and evaluations.
